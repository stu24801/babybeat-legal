FAQ

I can’t find our family invite code.
Open the app and go to the Family tab. The invite code is always shown there; you can copy it or share the invite link. If you are not yet a member and can’t reach the person who created the family, there is no other way to join, so please contact us.
Siri or the daycare sheet scan got something wrong.
After the AI processes a Siri “note” or a daycare sheet photo, you always see a “Confirm entries” screen first. You can edit or delete the time, type and amount of each entry before saving, without saying it again or retaking the photo. If you notice a mistake after saving, tap the entry in the timeline on the Today or Reports tab to edit it.
Does the daycare sheet scan send other children’s data anywhere?
No. Photos are recognized only on your iPhone and are never uploaded to our servers or any AI provider. If other children’s entries appear in the draft, delete them on the confirmation screen. See section 2 of the privacy policy for details.
Do the AI features cost money? Why download a model?
They are free. Voice logging, Siri “note” and daycare sheet scanning use an AI model downloaded to your iPhone, so nothing goes to the cloud and there is no usage limit. Download the model (about 2.6 GB) in Settings before first use; Wi-Fi is recommended. Manual entry and the fixed Siri commands (for example “BabyBeat, log pee”) don’t need the model.
What is the difference between owner, caregiver and viewer?
The owner can invite and remove members and manage baby profiles. Caregivers (for example parents or grandparents) can add and edit logs but can’t manage members. Viewers (for example relatives far away) can only see logs.
How do I leave a family or delete my account?
To leave a family: “Leave this family” at the bottom of the Family tab. To delete your account: Settings → Account → Delete Account. This can’t be undone. If you are an owner and other members remain, ownership passes to one of them and the logs stay with them; if you are the only member, the family and all logs are deleted.
