BabyBeat Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-16

BabyBeat (“the Service”) is provided by its developer as an individual (not a company). The Service helps caregivers log a baby’s feeds, diapers, sleep and pumping, and lets several caregivers in the same family (for example parents, grandparents or a nanny) share the same log in real time. By using the Service you agree to how this policy collects, processes and uses data.

Important: The Service records care information about a baby (usually under one year old, who cannot consent to data collection). This data is managed and consented to by the guardian (the caregiver using the Service) and is used only to help the family log and follow the baby’s care. It is not used for any other purpose.

We do not collect: daycare sheet photos (processed only on your iPhone, never sent to us or to any AI provider), your voice recordings (deleted right after they are turned into text), or access to your photo library (the app reads only the one photo you take or pick, and never scans your library).

2. Where AI features run

The Service uses AI to turn your voice, text or daycare sheet photos into draft care logs. All of this AI processing happens on your iPhone. The Service never sends your photos, recordings or logs to any AI provider.

  • Daycare sheet recognition: the photo is read by an AI model (Google Gemma 4) downloaded to your iPhone and is not uploaded to our servers. You review and edit the result before anything becomes a log.
  • In-app voice logging: the recording is turned into text on your iPhone, is not uploaded, and is deleted afterward.
  • Siri “note”: what you tell Siri is stored on your iPhone and turned into a draft by the on-device model when you open the app; it is not sent to a cloud AI. Siri’s speech-to-text is provided by Apple under Apple’s privacy policy.
  • Before first use, these features download a model of about 2.6 GB from Hugging Face. Like any website, Hugging Face sees your IP address during the download, but never receives your photos, recordings or logs.
  • The sections you mark on a photo and your format notes are stored only on that iPhone. They are not shared with family or uploaded.

Logs you confirm are stored on our servers (Amazon Web Services, Singapore region) so members of the same family can see them, just like logs entered by hand.

About daycare sheet photos: although photos never leave your iPhone, please photograph only your own child’s page. Other children’s entries on the photo are not uploaded but may appear in the draft; delete them on the confirmation screen.

3. How long we keep data

  • Voice: discarded right after recognition. Recordings are not kept.
  • Daycare sheet photos: processed only on your iPhone. Our systems never receive or store them. The app does not keep the photo, only the logs you confirm.
  • Care logs: kept with each entry so family members can look back, until you delete the entry or your account.

4. Your rights

You can view and correct data we hold about you or your family, delete any care log, leave a family, delete your account, or stop using the Service at any time.

Deleting your account: go to Settings → Account → Delete Account in the app. This cannot be undone. You leave every family and your sign-in account is deleted. If you own a family that has other members, ownership passes to one of them and the family’s logs stay with them. If you are the only member, the family, baby profiles and all logs are deleted.

For anything else, contact stu24801@gmail.com.

5. Security

The app does not hold keys to any cloud service. Photos and recordings are never uploaded, so they never appear in any system log. All data is transmitted over HTTPS/TLS.

6. Babies’ data and guardian consent

The users of the Service (the people who sign in and use the app) must be adult caregivers aged 18 or over who can agree to this policy. The app is not designed to be used by children. The content it records is care information about a baby (usually under one year old), entered, managed and consented to by the guardian (you). The family owner decides, through invitations and roles (owner / caregiver / viewer), who can see and add these logs.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. Significant changes will be announced in the app or on this page.

8. Contact

If you have any questions about this policy, contact stu24801@gmail.com.
